Privacy Policy: Mivchonan

App: "מבחונן – הכנה למבחן מחוננים ו׳" (Mivchonan) for Android and iOS
Effective date: 1.11.2026

In short: Mivchonan collects no data. There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no third-party services and no internet connection. Everything your child enters stays on the device, and we cannot see it.

1. Who we are

The app is developed and operated by Ben Shoham ("we"). For any privacy question, contact mivchonan.app@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store on servers, share or sell any personal or other information about users. The app has no server, and it does not request permission to access the internet.

3. What is stored on the device only

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on the device:

  • a name or nickname the child chooses, and a boy/girl choice so the Hebrew text uses the right grammatical gender;
  • the cartoon avatar the child builds;
  • practice progress: answers, levels, mistakes to review, stickers, ranks, streaks and an exam date if one was entered;
  • optional photos the user adds to the celebration pop-ups.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with the in-app reset in settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Photos: when you add a photo, the operating system's photo picker opens and the app receives only the photos you select. It gets no access to the rest of the gallery or to the camera. The selected photos are stored inside the app only.

Device backups: on Android, app data is excluded from device backups. On iPhone/iPad, if iCloud Backup is on, iOS may include the app's data in the backup kept in your own Apple account; Apple manages it under your settings and we have no access to it.

4. Permissions

The app does not request access to the internet, location, contacts, microphone or camera. On Android it uses only the vibration permission, for short haptic feedback in games.

5. Ads, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social features and no third-party SDKs. It contains no links to external websites, except in the parents' area, which is protected by a parental gate.

6. Purchases

If the app is sold for a price or offers an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive payment details or the buyer's identity. At most we see aggregated, non-identifying sales reports.

7. Children

The app is designed for children aged 9–12 and their parents. Because we collect no information at all, including from children, we do not ask for parental consent to collection. The app is built to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including its provisions on children, and Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981. It also follows Google Play's Families Policy and Apple's Kids Category requirements.

8. Your rights

All the information is on your device, so you can view, correct and delete it yourself at any time. We hold no copy to provide or erase. Questions: mivchonan.app@gmail.com.

9. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access, and is protected by the operating system's security. We recommend locking the device with a passcode.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, for example because a future feature needs data, we will update this page and the effective date before the app changes. A material change will also be shown in the app.

11. Disclaimer

Mivchonan is an independent product. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Israeli Ministry of Education, its Division for Gifted and Outstanding Students, or any testing institute.
